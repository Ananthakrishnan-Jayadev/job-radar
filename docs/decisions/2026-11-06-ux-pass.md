# Using the app day to day: grouping, companies, sorting, motion (2026-11-06)

A pass on the features already there, not new ones.

## Jobs page

- **One job posted for several places is one row.** Grouped by the same
  company and the same title (case and spaces ignored), within the current
  filters. The row shows a job you've already decided on if the group has
  one, else its first; "+6 places" counts distinct places (a place named by
  several postings shows "×7" in the job panel). One decision and one undo
  cover every posting in the group. Province counts count a group once per
  province, so the map agrees with the list. `group=0` on `/api/jobs` lists
  every posting; the CSV export and the CLI stay ungrouped.
  Different companies with the same title are never grouped.
- **Places cleaned.** Some boards send "Mississauga," or put the province in
  the city field ("Brampton, Ontario"). These are cleaned as they arrive, and
  the ones already stored are fixed once when the database is opened.
- **Back to your row** after opening a job's page (remembered per search for
  the browser tab).
- **Search words marked** in titles and descriptions; plain words match
  common endings ("engineer" marks "engineering"), `word*` any word start.
- **`?`** lists every key.
- **After a scan**, a line says how many new jobs it found under the default
  filters, with a link to just those (`new_since`).

## Companies

- **Remove companies**, one or many. Each removal first copies the companies
  with their boards, jobs, places and job history into
  `data/removed-companies-<date>.db`. Companies with tracked jobs are kept
  unless that's confirmed too. Seed or import can bring a company back.
- **Sort by any column heading** (also on the Applications tables); the
  first click sorts names A to Z and counts highest first.
- **Set one status on many** ticked companies.

## Motion and polish

Each action gets one short reply in the shared ease: the board cursor
slides between rows, the job panel settles in, bulk bars and confirm panels
drop in, rows slide when re-sorted and fade when removed, a status just set
glows once. Panels are flat, disabled main buttons are outlines, and the
thick coloured borders on the Applications and Scans cards are gone.
`DESIGN.md` records the motion rules.
